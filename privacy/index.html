Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Saylex is a vocabulary app for Android made by Aaron Dave D. Callanga ("we" on this page). This page explains what data the app uses and where that data goes.

What stays on your phone

Everything you write in Saylex is stored only on your device: your level, your streak, your sentences, your lexicon and your theme setting. We do not have accounts, and we do not copy this data to any server. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What leaves your phone

Only one feature sends data off your device: Improve.

When you tap Improve, the app sends today's word and the sentence you wrote to our server, which runs on Cloudflare Workers. The server asks an AI model hosted by Cloudflare (Workers AI) for a corrected version and returns it to your phone.

  • We use the sentence only to answer that request.
  • Our server does not save it, log it or link it to you.
  • The connection is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • If you never tap Improve, nothing you write leaves your phone.

Like any website, our server briefly sees your IP address so it can respond and limit abuse (no more than a few requests per minute). We do not store it.

Cloudflare handles this as our service provider. You can read Cloudflare's own policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Word lists may also be downloaded from our server so the app can get new words. These downloads do not include any of your data.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • No account, email or contact list access.

Children

Saylex is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
